**Description** (App Store and Google Play, ≤ 4000)

> Alpha Planner brings a Pomodoro focus timer, a task manager and a little game together, so getting things done feels like progress you can see.
>
> **Focus with the Pomodoro technique**
> • Focus sessions, short and long breaks, with the lengths you choose
> • Alerts at the end of every session and break, even with the screen off or the app closed
> • Link a task to the timer and see how many sessions it took
> • Optional auto-start for breaks and sessions, keep-screen-awake while you focus
>
> **Plan your tasks**
> • Tasks with subtasks, priorities, categories, due dates and due-time reminders
> • Four views: list, kanban board, calendar and timeline
> • Search, filter by category and priority, sort by due date, priority or newest
>
> **Stay motivated**
> • Earn XP and climb 50 levels
> • 81 achievements to unlock, from your first focus session to hidden ones
> • Daily quests, weekly goals (including one you set yourself) and day streaks
> • Session and daily combos, and a monthly season with its own rewards
> • A short summary of your day
>
> **Private by design**
> • No account, no login, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
> • Everything stays on your device; export it to a file whenever you like
> • Free and open source (MIT license): read the code on GitHub
>
> **Made for everyone**
> • English and Turkish
> • Light and dark mode
> • Works with VoiceOver and TalkBack, large text and Reduce Motion
>
> Alpha Planner is free, with no in-app purchases.

**What's new in 1.0.0** (App Store "What's New" ≤ 4000, Play release notes ≤ 500) — 217 characters
First release: Pomodoro timer with reliable alerts, tasks in list, kanban, calendar and timeline views, XP, levels, achievements, quests, goals and seasons. Local-first and private: no account, all data on your phone.
