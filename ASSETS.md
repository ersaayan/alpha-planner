### The Alpha Planner logo

The logo (a smiling green planner with check marks) was generated by the maintainer with **Google Gemini** and then cropped, resized and given a solid background for the app icon, adaptive icon, monochrome icon and splash screen.

- It is **not** covered by the MIT licence of the source code.
- Google does not claim ownership of content generated with its services ([Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms)). Google may generate the same or similar images for other users.
- Images generated entirely by AI may not be protected by copyright in some jurisdictions. Regardless of that, the logo is the identity of the published Alpha Planner app: **forks distributed under another name must replace the app icon and splash art** (see [Forking and publishing your own build](README.md#forking-and-publishing-your-own-build)).
