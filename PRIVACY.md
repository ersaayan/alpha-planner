# Privacy Policy

**Alpha Planner** · Last updated: 17 October 2026

Alpha Planner is a free, open-source Pomodoro timer and task manager. It is built to be **local-first**: everything you create stays on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice. It applies to the app as published on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

## The short version

- **No account.** You do not sign up or log in.
- **No data collection.** We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data.
- **No analytics, no ads, no tracking.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not build a profile of you.
- **All data stays on your device.** Your tasks, categories, timer history, settings and progress are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone.
- **Notifications are local.** Timer alerts are scheduled by your device itself. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

## Data the app stores on your device

To do its job, the app keeps the following on your device only:

- the tasks, subtasks, categories and due dates you create;
- your Pomodoro settings and session history;
- your progress: XP, level, streaks, achievements, quests and goals;
- your app preferences, such as theme and language.

We never see this data and it is not transmitted anywhere by the app.

If you use **Settings → Data → Export**, the app writes your data to a file and hands it to your phone's share sheet; where that file goes (Files, Drive, a message…) is your choice and is not visible to us. **Import** reads a file you pick and replaces the data on the device.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** used to alert you when a focus session or break ends, and optionally to show the running timer. These are *local* notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in your system settings.
- **Background activity, sound, vibration and keeping the device awake:** used only so the timer can alert you reliably when the screen is off or the app is in the background.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

## Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it:

- use **Reset All Data** in the app's Settings to erase your tasks, progress and settings; or
- delete the app, which removes its local data from the device.

Your operating system's own device backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include app data, depending on your device settings. That is controlled by your device and your backup provider, not by Alpha Planner.

## Children

Alpha Planner is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Builds with optional sync (self-hosters)

The source code contains an **optional, experimental** sync feature that is turned off in the app published in the stores. People who build their own version from source can switch it on by supplying their own Supabase backend. Such builds send data to a server run by whoever distributes that build, so they are **covered by that distributor's own privacy policy, not by this one.** This policy describes only the store version, which has no server component.

## Open source

Alpha Planner's code is public at [github.com/ersaayan/alpha-planner](https://github.com/ersaayan/alpha-planner), so you can verify these statements yourself.
